Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about getting started, supported devices and what you can enjoy on StreamlyTV.

What devices can I use?
StreamlyTV supports Fire TV devices, Android TV and Google TV, Samsung and LG TVs, Apple TV, Chromecast, Android phones and tablets, iPhone and iPad, Windows PCs and Mac computers.
Can I use Smart TV?
Yes. Samsung and LG Smart TVs and Android TV or Google TV devices work with a compatible IPTV player.
Can I use Firestick?
Yes. Fire TV Stick HD, 4K, 4K Max and 4K Plus are supported. The Fire TV Stick 4K Max is recommended for best performance. Contact support for help choosing and setting up an IPTV player.
Can I use iPhone or Android?
Yes. StreamlyTV works on iPhone, iPad, Android phones and Android tablets with a compatible IPTV player.
Can I use Apple TV?
Yes. Apple TV is supported using a compatible IPTV player.
Can I use Chromecast?
Yes. Chromecast is supported with a compatible IPTV player.
Can I use mobile data?
Yes. Wi-Fi, Ethernet and 4G/5G mobile data can work. More than 50 Mbps is recommended for the best experience, especially for HD, FHD and 4K.
Do I need a VPN?
No. A VPN is not required to use StreamlyTV.
Can I use more than one device?
You can install the app on multiple devices, but only one active stream is supported at a time. Starting a second stream may stop or log out the first.
Is there a free trial?
Yes. A 24-hour free trial is available. Request yours on the Contact page.
How does setup work?
Choose a package or request a trial, then contact StreamlyTV on WhatsApp. The team will collect the details needed for your device and app, complete the setup and let you know when it is ready.
Are movies and series included?
Yes. Movies and series are included, with HD, FHD and 4K quality where available. Some titles offer subtitles or dubbed audio, but not all.
Are live sports included?
Sports and live events are available, including football, UFC and other sports categories. Channels and event availability can change as the library is updated.
Are subtitles available?
Live TV does not include subtitles. Some movies and series have subtitles or dubbed audio, but availability varies by title.
Is adult content included?
No. StreamlyTV does not provide adult content.
